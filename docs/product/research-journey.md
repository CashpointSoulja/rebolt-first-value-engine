# Research and journey evidence

**Independent concept by Ayo Ahmed. Not affiliated with Rebolt.** Evidence date: **11 October 2026**. All proposed events, analysis and experiments are synthetic.

## Method and evidence classes

Direct research here consists only of GET retrievals of the exact public role page, official root and official pilot guide. Only targeted excerpts are retained below, not raw HTML, contact details, authentication material or footer/social links. No authenticated workspace was inspected. Public marketing content is not independent validation of capabilities or outcomes.

The owner-reported observations were supplied as deidentified context. They were **not reproduced by direct research** and do not establish universal eligibility rules, a complete signup funnel or absent product features. The original research bundle records its own earlier no-form-submission boundary; that history is unchanged.

## Journey map: do not bridge evidence gaps

| Journey step | Evidence / uncertainty | Workbench representation |
|---|---|---|
| Discover public offer | Official aviation page and role, R01–R02 | Frozen synthetic acquisition `channel`; no visitor, spend or campaign-delivery dataset |
| Express interest / qualification | Owner report O01–O03 | Hypothetical `qualified_lead`; reported thank-you is not equated with an account or first value |
| Agree use case, owner and timeline | Public pilot guidance R03; actual handoff unknown | Hypothetical `pilot_setup`; no scheduling, invitation or operational action |
| First product use | No authenticated use observed | Hypothetical `first_use`; explicitly not proof of value |
| Complete a use-case result with value evidence | Proposed milestone; actual Rebolt definition unknown | `first_value_verified` requires completion plus one approved synthetic evidence category |
| Commercial decision / billing | Role scope is public; actual journey/payment evidence unknown | Discuss as a future research question, not an implemented paid conversion metric |

The implementation's unit is a **synthetic account**, chosen for a reproducible demonstration. It is not claimed to be Rebolt's internal entity model. The seven-day milestone window is unrelated to the official four-to-six-week pilot guidance.

## Research questions still open

1. What entity, eligibility policy and authoritative event would a real product owner choose?
2. What completed use-case result would count as value, and who could verify the evidence?
3. What trusted coverage/watermark would distinguish missing outcomes from non-conversion?
4. Where do qualification, assisted setup and authenticated first use begin and end?
5. What separate entry population and verified payment evidence would support a billing metric?

No additional account creation, outreach, application submission, payment or operational research is authorized by these questions.
