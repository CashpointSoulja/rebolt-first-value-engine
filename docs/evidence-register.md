## Method and provenance

Bounded official page and linked-asset GETs, rendered desktop/mobile screenshots, computed styles, source stylesheet preservation, DOM SVG extraction, rendered-font inspection and reduced-motion media emulation. No forms were submitted. No personal/customer datasets, private browser state or secrets are part of the bundle.

The [manifest](research/source-manifest.json) records URL, bytes and SHA-256 for preserved official CSS/SVG files. [Official editorial copy](research/official-copy.json) and [font declarations](research/font-declarations.json) retain public-source evidence only. Screenshots show public marketing, not native product behavior.

## Claim policy

Use “Rebolt's page describes/advertises…” for C-class statements. Use “proposed/hypothesis…” for P/H statements. Never say this concept fixes a missing Rebolt feature, improves real conversion, integrates with real systems or produces safe aircraft instructions. A number in synthetic analysis is neither customer performance nor a business benchmark.
